See every Windows change before it happens. Undo it after.

DIVoptimizer scans your PC, recommends only what fits your hardware, shows the exact current and new value of each setting, backs up what it touches, and restores it on request.

irm YOUR-DOMAIN/run | iex

Paste into PowerShell. It opens a window where you choose what to apply. Nothing changes until you approve it.

What happens when you run it

  1. ScanReads your hardware and settings. Changes nothing.
  2. RecommendAdvice differs for a laptop, a desktop, 8 GB or 32 GB.
  3. PreviewCurrent → new, risk level, downside, restart needed.
  4. Back upVerified before anything is touched.
  5. ApplyOnly what you approved. Each change is verified.
  6. RestoreFrom the app, or from the emergency reset script.

Before you run it

What it does not promise.

DIVoptimizer does not guarantee FPS increases, lower latency, lower temperatures, or faster Windows performance. Results depend on hardware, drivers, applications, configuration, thermals and workload.

Read it first. The command downloads a script and runs it with your privileges. The full source is on GitHub; read it, or pin a reviewed version instead of the latest.

Defender and Windows Update are never part of any profile. Removing apps is not automatically reversible, and the app says so before you confirm.
